### 🚀 About Me

I am a Computer Engineering student passionate about the synergy between **hardware architecture** and **systems software**. My work revolves around exploring how tailored hardware and specialized processors can accelerate compute-intensive workloads and AI algorithms.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Processor microarchitecture, hardware/software co-design, and embedded systems.
- 💡 **Research Interests:** Domain-Specific Accelerators (DSA), RISC-V ecosystem, and high-performance digital design.
- 🎓 **Education:** B.Sc. in Computer Engineering, University of Tehran.
